# Shopwatch

Shopwatch helps a household watch products and compare Australian buying options in AUD.

## Language

**Product**:
An exact model and variant a household is considering buying. A separately watched variant is a separate product with its own card.
_Avoid_: Product family

**Household watchlist**:
The shared collection of products watched in a private Shopwatch app. A product remains watched while its card remains, including after a purchase; removing the card stops watching it.

**Local area**:
The Australian postcode used to assess nearby pickup options and delivery availability.

**Delivered price**:
The total price of a buying option including shipping and applicable discounts. Unknown shipping remains explicitly unresolved.

**Buying advice**:
An evidence-based assessment of buying now or waiting, with dates and uncertainty made explicit. Missing history is an unknown, and seasonal sales do not guarantee a discount.

**Listing**:
A buying option for an exact product from a particular retailer or marketplace seller. A saved link can remain a listing even when automatic price checking is unavailable.

**Price check**:
An attempt to refresh a watched product's listing prices. Checks can run on a schedule, and an unsuccessful check does not establish a new price.

**Target-price alert**:
A notification that a confirmed comparable price meets the household's chosen target for a product.

**Price-drop alert**:
An optional notification of a confirmed decrease in a comparable price. Repeated checks at the same price do not constitute another drop.

**Buying mode**:
The household's intent for a watched product: “just watching” for future opportunities, or “buy ASAP” for a purchase wanted now.
